### GEODNET (GEOD; Muse 69/100; WATCH, investable instrument but not a buy)
- [Coinbase GEOD page](https://www.coinbase.com/price/geodnet) explicitly says GEOD is available on its centralized exchange. Nov 16 page shows ~$0.20, 481,457,634 circulating and ~959,458,068 total. Market values change; this is a dated snapshot, not a quote.
- [DefiLlama GEODNET](https://defillama.com/protocol/geodnet?events=false&revenue=true&tvl=false) reports monthly revenue/fees and buybacks, **but the measurement is circular**: fees are modeled from token burns divided by a presumed percentage, not independently audited fiat sales. Therefore Muse's $2.78M Q3 'revenue' is **not independently verified operating revenue**.
- [Dropresolve buyback investigation checked Nov 16](https://www.dropresolve.com/token/geodnet) observes burns but not same-transaction swaps, publicly labelled purchase wallets, or proof of customer cash funding them. Absence of a swap in a burn transaction **does not prove there was no earlier purchase**, but claimed economic linkage is unproven.
- Outstanding: contradictory 80%-vs-40% buyback descriptions, Coinbase vs other providers circulating supply, unlocks, emissions, station churn, customer cash receipts, exact Polygon/Solana custody network. Token holders have no corporate equity/dividend. **No buy.**
- US retail $100–$500: On [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/how-to-buy/iotube-bridged-geod-iotex), verify account and asset GEOD, fund USD, select GEOD spot, inspect order preview, prefer limit order if supported, confirm fee/spread, buy only if owner decides. Approx 500–2,500 GEOD at $0.20 *before fees*; platform availability may vary by account/state. Custodial exchange and thin-liquidity risks. Avoid bridge/wrapped lookalikes.

### Fervo Energy (FRVO; Muse 67/100; WATCH, investable instrument but not a buy)
- [Fervo investor relations](https://ir.fervoenergy.com/) lists **Nov 9, 2026 Q3 results**. Company reports GeoBlock 1 33 MW commercial operation, Q3 revenue ~$116K, operating loss ~$43.7M, $1.9B cash, contracted multi-year power sales and substantial future capital needs. The lifetime backlog is **not current sales or guaranteed profit**. These are company-reported figures, not an independent plant audit.
- Reservoir performance and short-seller allegations remain **disputed**. Need actual capacity factor, produced MWh, water losses, temperature trend, GeoBlocks 2–3 commissioning, financing terms, and 19 Nov Cape Station investor event.
- US retail $100–$500: company investor site identifies Nasdaq FRVO. Verify symbol in a regulated US brokerage; open/fund account, search **FRVO**, verify issuer, check live bid/ask and fractional-share eligibility, use limit order or fractional buy (if broker supports), review fees and order, only then confirm if owner chooses. $100–$500 nominal exposure possible; share count depends on live price. Equity ownership participates in upside but carries dilution, financing, technology and total-loss risk. No purchase recommended.
