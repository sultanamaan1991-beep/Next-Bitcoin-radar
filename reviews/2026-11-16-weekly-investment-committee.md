## Independent challenge and source status

**GEODNET:** [GEODNET's own Blockworks Q4 2024 disclosure](https://app.blockworks.com/projects/geodnet/institutional-relations/q4-2024) says **80% of correction-data revenue** buys and burns GEOD, while a later source cited by Muse uses a 50%-to-foundation then 80%-of-that formulation (~40% of subscription revenue). This could reflect different revenue bases or a changed policy; **do not assert the contradiction is conclusively resolved**. [DefiLlama-derived tracker](https://www.tokenbuybacks.app/token/geod/) reports ~$763K 30-day burns/buybacks, but explicitly estimates revenue using a presumed buyback fraction; thus it is **not independent proof of customer fiat receipts or exchange purchases**. [Community statements](https://www.reddit.com/r/GeodNet/comments/1j2b89w) repeat the 80% claim, not transaction-level proof. The absence of an on-chain swap in the *burn* transaction alone does not prove no purchase occurred earlier or off-chain. Need labeled wallet funding trail, exchange fills, independently audited cash receipts and canonical supply reconciliation. Token holders own no company equity/dividend. Competition: Point One, Trimble, Swift, free public RTK/CORS; station economics can deteriorate as rewards halve. Failure signals: reward-driven node attrition, token unlock pressure, burns unrelated to customer revenue, enterprise customers failing to renew.

**Fervo:** [Company Nov 9 release](https://ir.fervoenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fervo-energy-reports-third-quarter-2026-results) and [Nasdaq confirmation Oct 30](https://ir.fervoenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fervo-energy-report-third-quarter-2026-results-november-9-2026) support FRVO listing, 33 MW commercial block and financial figures. [Reuters May 13 IPO](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/fervo-energy-valued-1021-billion-shares-rise-nasdaq-debut-2026-05-13/) corroborates Nasdaq debut and IPO at $27. Critical: $11.9B is lifetime contracted potential, not current sales or margin. The CEO pilot-temperature-decline remark is **transcript-reported** in Muse pack; full original transcript and regulator/engineering data not independently checked in this review. Short-seller magnitude and reservoir-life allegations remain **DISPUTED**; do not treat them as established. Verify GeoBlock 1 sustained MWh/capacity factor, water loss, temperature, GeoBlocks 2/3 by Jan 1, 2027, project-financing terms, dilution and Nov 19 open house. Competitors: conventional geothermal, natural gas, nuclear, other EGS developers. Failure: poor sustained output, excessive capex or failed project finance.
