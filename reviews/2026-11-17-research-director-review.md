## Why this matters by 2031–2036
AI-designed enzymes and gene editors could become industrial biotech infrastructure; geothermal generation and shallow geothermal drilling could enable always-on power and cooling; robot welding and orbital drug development could become new production platforms; machine payments could support autonomous software commerce; decentralized centimeter-grade positioning could serve robotics and logistics. These are **technology scenarios**, not price forecasts or investment theses until evidence and ownership economics are verified.

## Red-team / failure signals
- GEOD: no audited real customer receipts, unclear supply definitions, net token inflation, token buybacks not tied to revenue.
- FRVO: reservoir output below contracts, capital markets closed, delays or substantial dilution.
- Biomatter: customers are only pilots or revenue does not scale despite delivered enzymes; competition from in-house AI protein teams.
- Bedrock: drilling advantage fails independent test; installations don't translate to positive unit economics; competition from traditional geothermal HVAC.
- Profluent: no commercial capture from open science or no clinical program.
- Varda: spacecraft returns fail to produce profitable pharmaceutical output.
- x402: agent traffic is promotional/wash or protocol usage does not create investable equity/token.
