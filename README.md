## Research rules

- Look for overlooked 2026-stage builders and actual customer adoption, not hype or mature proxy stocks.
- Distinguish **source-reported**, **independently verified**, **unverified**, and **disputed** facts.
- For each candidate, track real US retail access for **$100–$500**, ownership rights, valuation, dilution, token economics, and downside.
- Private or inaccessible companies remain **NOT INVESTABLE** for this mission even when the technology is compelling.
- Funding rounds, price drops and large theoretical backlogs do not prove investment value.
- Keep dated source links, preserve past assessments, and never invent quotes, tokens, contracts or returns.
- GitHub changes must reflect what was actually checked. No automatic purchases.
